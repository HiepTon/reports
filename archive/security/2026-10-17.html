User refused to change her password because everyone already knew it

Summary

A story from a UK school's IT support highlights a severe password policy issue where a data manager refused to change her password because 'everyone knew it,' even writing it on a piece of paper taped to her desk. This occurred after a ransomware attack, and the principal eventually ordered the IT staff to revert the new, stronger password policy.

Analysis

This anecdote illustrates a persistent and dangerous human factor in cybersecurity: resistance to basic security practices. Defenders must implement strong, enforced password policies and provide ongoing, effective security awareness training. Hunting for weak password usage, especially in systems that have experienced past breaches, should be a continuous effort.
